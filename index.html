Innovation is at the heart of everything we do. At ASGT, we believe technology should do more than solve today’s challenges; it should create new possibilities for tomorrow. From initial briefing to handover.

Engagements typically take one of four forms: fixed-scope delivery, a dedicated team, a systems audit and recovery, or a security assessment.

  1. BriefProblem definition, constraints and success criteria.
  2. Scoping noteApproach, timeline, cost and assumptions documented in writing.
  3. BuildWorking software delivered in testable increments.
  4. VerifyTesting, security review and acceptance.
  5. HandoverSource code, documentation and support. You retain full ownership.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of projects do you take on?

We undertake selected client engagements: new products and applications, work on existing systems, AI and data projects, and security assessments.

Who owns the code?

You do. Handover includes source code, documentation and support, and everything we build for you is yours.

How do you agree scope and price?

Following an initial discussion of the problem, we issue a written scoping note covering approach, timeline, cost and assumptions before any build begins.

Do you work with clients outside Lagos?

Yes. We are based in Delta, Lagos, Nigeria and London, UK and work with clients internationally.

How does a security engagement work?

We agree targets, boundaries and authorisation in writing, then test and report each finding with its impact and recommended fix. We retest afterwards to confirm that remediation is effective.

How quickly will you reply?

We respond to every enquiry, typically within two working days.
